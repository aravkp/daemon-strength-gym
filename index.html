The Approach

Not your typical
commercial gym.

Daemon Strength was opened by Manoj Datic — a former senior-level cricketer described in our reviews as the fastest bowler in Rajasthan — after fifteen years inside competitive sport and strength coaching. He built a studio for people who were tired of crowded floors and templated workouts.

Every program here is one-on-one. We start by understanding how your body actually moves: where it's tight, where it leaks force, what previous injuries left behind. Then we build a plan around it — strength, rehab, sport, or fat loss — and grade it up, week by week.

The studio is co-owned by Kiran ma'am, who runs dedicated personal training for women — a female-friendly environment that members credit for finally making the gym feel like a place they belong.
